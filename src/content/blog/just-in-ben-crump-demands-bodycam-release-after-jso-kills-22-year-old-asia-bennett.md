Another Black life taken by law enforcement, and once again the department is playing games with basic transparency. Jacksonville Sheriff's Office officers shot and killed 22-year-old Asia Bennett, and civil rights attorney Ben Crump is stepping in to demand the footage they are desperately trying to stall.

> Asia Bennett, 22, was shot and killed by a Jacksonville Sheriff's Office (JSO) officer on Aug. 11. Attorney Crump will demand that JSO release the body camera and any dash camera footage from the shooting.
> 
> — [Ben Crump](https://x.com/AttorneyCrump/status/2090455090666557576)

We all know the playbook by now: if the video exonerated the cop, it would have been released in high definition before the end of the shift.
