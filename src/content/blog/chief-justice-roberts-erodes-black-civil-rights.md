Reflecting on Chief Justice John Roberts requires examining a judicial legacy that has systematically dismantled critical Black civil rights protections, from voting rights to affirmative action, while echoing historical precedents like Plessy v. Ferguson that curtailed Black freedom and equality.

> When examining the judicial trajectory of Chief Justice John Roberts, civil rights advocates must confront a record that fundamentally reshaped American jurisprudence at the expense of Black communities. From gutting the Voting Rights Act in Shelby County v. Holder to striking down affirmative action in higher education, the Roberts Court has consistently rolled back key protections hard-won during the Civil Rights Movement. Meanwhile, looking back at historical figures like Homer Plessy reminds us of the long, painful struggle against judicial rulings that institutionalized racial inequality across the nation.
