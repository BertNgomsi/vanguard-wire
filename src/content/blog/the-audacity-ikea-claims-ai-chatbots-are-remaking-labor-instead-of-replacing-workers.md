> After rolling out a customer service bot, the Swedish retailer is remaking—not replacing—its workforce.
> 
> — [Fortune (Race & Business)](https://fortune.com/2026/07/30/ikea-ai-workforce-reskilling-jobs-billie-chatbot-global-500/)
