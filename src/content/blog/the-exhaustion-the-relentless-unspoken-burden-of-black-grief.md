America loves the 'resilient Black person' trope because it means society never has to apologize for the trauma it inflicts on our communities. Whether it's state violence, systemic health disparities, or the sheer stress of existing while Black, unexpected loss is a crushing weight we are expected to quietly swallow. It's time to reject the capitalist demand that we always remain unbreakable.

> Alexis K. Ellis writes about the pain of unexpectedly losing a loved one and encourages people to take time to mourn and grieve.
> 
> — [Mississippi Free Press](https://www.mississippifreepress.org/opinion-coping-with-unexpectedly-losing-a-loved-one/)

Refuse the mainstream expectation to just wipe your tears and get back to work; allowing yourself to fall apart in a system designed to destroy you is a radical act of defiance.
