Traditional banking was designed around rigid, outdated assumptions that leave gig workers, freelancers, and Black and brown communities stranded. A former bank exec is finally calling out the system's structural flaws and launching a venture meant to rewrite the rules.

> The financial system was built for a world where everyone had one employer and a paycheck that landed every two weeks. That world is disappearing.
> 
> — [Fortune (Race & Business)](https://fortune.com/2026/08/22/james-francisco-turner-lazaro-banc-of-california-new-startup/)

Mainstream banks have spent decades shutting out gig workers and marginalized communities, so watching a banker pivot to fix the mess is a welcome change.
