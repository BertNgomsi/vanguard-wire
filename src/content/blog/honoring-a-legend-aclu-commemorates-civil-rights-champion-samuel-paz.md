While mainstream media routinely ignores the legal warriors fighting police misconduct, we recognize those who dedicated their lives to defending Black and Brown communities against state violence. Samuel Paz was a relentless champion for civil rights.

> The ACLU commemorates former board member Samuel Paz, a dedicated civil rights attorney who spent decades fighting police misconduct, defending constitutional rights, and challenging systemic injustice across Southern California to ensure equal protection under the law for marginalized and vulnerable communities throughout his distinguished legal career and lifetime of public service.
> 
> — [ACLU National News & Press](https://www.aclu.org/press-releases/aclu-commemorates-samuel-paz-former-board-member)

His legal legacy remains a vital blueprint for holding law enforcement accountable in an era that desperately needs it.
