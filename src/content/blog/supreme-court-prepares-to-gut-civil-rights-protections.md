When SCOTUS starts chipping away at sex discrimination laws, Black women take the direct hit. Don’t fall for the sanitized legal jargon—gutting these protections is just another weapon in the High Court’s ongoing crusade to dismantle all civil rights defenses.

> Weakening the protection against sex discrimination? The Supreme Court continues to reexamine long-standing precedents under Title VII and civil rights statutes. Opponents argue that narrowing the definition of discriminatory harm creates higher hurdles for plaintiffs seeking relief in federal courts. Critics warn that any erosion of federal anti-discrimination safeguards inevitably dismantles vital legal protections for marginalized employees across the country, leaving workers vulnerable to systemic bias and institutional hostility without adequate judicial recourse or accountability from employers.
