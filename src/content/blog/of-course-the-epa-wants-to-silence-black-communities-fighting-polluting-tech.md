Because letting massive tech corporations choke our neighborhoods with smog wasn't enough, the EPA is now quietly trying to gag us, too. While Silicon Valley builds massive, energy-guzzling data centers in our backyards, the federal government wants to make public pushback "optional." So much for environmental justice.

> From Memphis to Bessemer, public permit records and hearings have helped Black communities discover what enormous data centers could mean for their air — and organize to fight back. Public hearings and permit records have helped communities challenge massive data centers and their pollution. Now, the EPA is considering making that public participation optional, potentially weakening one of residents’ few opportunities to push back.
> 
> — [Word in Black](https://wordinblack.com/2026/09/black-communities-could-lose-a-key-weapon/)
