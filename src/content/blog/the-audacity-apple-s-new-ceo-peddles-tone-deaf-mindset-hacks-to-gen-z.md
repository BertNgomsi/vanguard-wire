Another day, another tech multi-millionaire offering up regurgitated hustle-culture drivel to a generation drowning in debt and gig-economy scraps. Apple's freshly minted CEO John Ternus thinks the secret to surviving late-stage capitalism is just a little mindset hack, completely ignoring the systemic barriers that actually dictate who gets to climb the corporate ladder at a multi-trillion dollar monopoly.

> Apple CEO John Ternus shared the mindset hack that steered his 25-year career at the $4.6 trillion tech giant: 'Assume you’re as smart as anyone else.'
> 
> — [Fortune (Race & Business)](https://fortune.com/2026/09/01/apple-new-ceo-john-ternus-told-gen-z-never-assume-they-know-as-much-as-others-mindset-hack-push-forward/)

Sure, John. Because sheer confidence and a mindset hack are definitely all it takes for marginalized folks to shatter the concrete ceilings of Silicon Valley's notoriously white, male-dominated C-suites.
