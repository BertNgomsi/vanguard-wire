Long before Steve Harvey was giving viral reaction faces on daytime game shows, he was delivering top-tier 90s comedy alongside Cedric the Entertainer. Thirty years after its premiere, it's time to put some respect on a classic sitcom that anchored Black TV culture.

> Today marks 30 years since The Steve Harvey Show premiered on August 25, 1996. The WB sitcom introduced viewers to Steve Hightower, a former funk musician who finds a new career teaching music at Booker T. Washington High School on Chicago’s West Side.
> 
> — [Essence (News & Politics)](https://www.essence.com/entertainment/the-steve-harvey-show-30-then-and-now/)

They truly do not make classic Black ensemble comedies like this anymore, so put some respect on the High Tops.
