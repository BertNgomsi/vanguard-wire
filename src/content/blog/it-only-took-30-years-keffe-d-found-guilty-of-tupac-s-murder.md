It only took Las Vegas police nearly three decades and a highly publicized memoir where the suspect literally confessed, but the justice system finally managed to secure a conviction for the murder of Tupac Shakur. Duane 'Keffe D' Davis has been found guilty, closing the longest-running open secret in hip-hop history.

> Nearly 30 years after the murder of Tupac Shakur, a Las Vegas jury has found Duane “Keffe D” Davis guilty of first-degree murder in connection with the 1996 killing of the hip-hop icon. Jurors returned the unanimous verdict Monday, Aug. 31, after deliberating for less than three hours following a multi-week trial.
> 
> — [NewsOne](https://newsone.com/6870738/keffe-d-guilty-tupac-shakur-murder-trial/)
