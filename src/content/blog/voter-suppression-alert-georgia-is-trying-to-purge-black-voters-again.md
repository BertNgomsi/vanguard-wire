Philip Randolph Institute, Common Cause Georgia, and League of Women Voters of Georgia, represented by Advancement Project, Lawyers’ Committee for Civil Rights Under Law, Southern Poverty Law Center, NAACP move to intervene in a suit to protect 5,000 Georgia voters at risk of removal from the November election rolls.
